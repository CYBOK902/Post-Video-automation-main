# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 20, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to TikTok Video Automation. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our platform.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and what choices you have.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

### Account information

- TikTok username and display name
- TikTok user ID
- Basic profile information authorized through TikTok

### Video information

- Videos uploaded by users
- Video captions and descriptions
- Publishing preferences
- Publishing status

### Technical information

- Browser and device information
- IP address and basic usage information
- Application activity and error logs

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use information only as necessary to:

- Connect users' TikTok accounts
- Authenticate users
- Manage uploaded videos
- Process video publishing requests
- Monitor application performance and resolve technical problems
- Maintain application security

## 4. TikTok API Usage

Our application integrates with TikTok through official TikTok for Developers products and APIs.

Users authenticate through TikTok Login Kit and authorize the permissions displayed during TikTok's authorization process. After connecting an account, users may select or upload video content, prepare captions, configure publishing settings, and initiate publishing through TikTok's Content Posting API.

The application uses requested permissions only to facilitate video publishing on behalf of authenticated users. Users remain in control of their content and must authorize the publishing process.

We do not use TikTok information for purposes unrelated to providing the requested functionality. Our use of TikTok data is subject to applicable TikTok Developer Terms and policies.

## 5. Information Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that support hosting, infrastructure, or other necessary application operations. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect the security and rights of users and our service.

## 6. Data Storage and Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

TikTok access tokens must be handled securely and must not be exposed publicly. We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal requirements, and fulfill legitimate operational purposes.

## 7. User Rights and Data Deletion

Users may:

- Disconnect their TikTok accounts
- Revoke application permissions through TikTok
- Request deletion of their personal information
- Request information about data associated with their account

To request account or personal data deletion, contact us by email. We will process valid deletion requests in accordance with applicable law and TikTok policies.

## 8. Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for authentication, session management, security, and website functionality. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

## 9. Third-Party Services

Our application may interact with TikTok and other third-party services. These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Our platform is not intended for children below the minimum age required to use the service under applicable laws and TikTok policies. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.
